Privacy Policy

Welcome to The Tree Team. We build utility apps for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information our apps may access, how app permissions are used, and how ads are served through Google AdMob.

Permissions We Use

Our apps may request Android permissions only when they are needed for app features, purchases, storage access, network access, or advertising support. Some permissions may not appear on every device or in every app version.

You can grant or revoke app permissions at any time from your Android device settings. If you revoke a permission, some features may stop working correctly.

The permissions currently used by our apps may include:

  • Internet access for app features, updates, and Google AdMob adsandroid.permission.INTERNET
  • Device or cellular network state when required by specific app featuresandroid.permission.READ_PHONE_STATE
  • Shared storage access for reading, saving, importing, or exporting user-selected filesandroid.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
  • Google Play Billing for in-app purchases or premium featurescom.android.vending.BILLING

Data We Collect

We do not require users to create an account, and we do not directly collect names, email addresses, passwords, or precise location data through this website or through our utility apps unless a specific app feature clearly asks the user to provide that information.

Our apps use Google AdMob to display ads. Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share information such as IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, Android Advertising ID, app set ID, and other device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

The Tree Team does not store the Android Advertising ID on our own servers. Advertising data is handled by Google AdMob according to Google policies and user ad settings.

Users can control personalized ads from Android or Google account settings. Users may also reset or delete their advertising ID where supported by their Android version.

When a user selects, imports, exports, or saves files using an app feature, the app may access only the files needed for that action. We do not use storage access to collect unrelated personal files.

Third-Party Advertising

Our apps use Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google. AdMob may use identifiers, cookies, or similar technologies to deliver, limit, measure, and improve ads.

For more information about how Google uses data, please visit Google's partner sites policy and Google Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through one of our apps, please contact us so we can review and delete it where applicable.

Security

We design our apps to limit the information they access and to protect data during normal use. Advertising data is handled by Google AdMob under Google's security practices, and files selected by users stay on their device unless an app feature clearly requires sharing or exporting them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the updated version will apply from the date it is published.
